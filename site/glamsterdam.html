Would this transaction survive Glamsterdam?

Paste a mainnet transaction hash. Your browser asks a public node for the transaction, its block's header and the pre-state it ran on (debug_traceTransaction with the prestate tracer), then runs it in fevm, the EVM written in Fe, compiled to WebAssembly: under Osaka, as it ran, and under Glamsterdam's execution rules (Config::amsterdam(): EIP-8037 and EIP-8038 state gas, EIP-2780, EIP-7976 and EIP-7981 repricing), with the gas limit it was sent with. If it would fail, fevm searches for the least gas limit that would do. On 93 mainnet blocks, 35% of transactions would fail as sent, and geth's Amsterdam rules agree with fevm's on all 3,718 compared (the numbers).

Examples from block 26,424,805: a transfer to a new account, sent with 21,000 gas, a contract call.
