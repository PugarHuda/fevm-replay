Where two runs part

Each transaction below is real: fevm ran it on its mainnet pre-state twice, once with the deployed code (the Uniswap V2 pair, or USDT) and once with the Fe port in its place. Every call is listed in the order it began, indented by depth. The call where the runs first part, in the order calls ended, is marked. Pick a call for its calldata and output. The data comes from fevm_replay trace.
